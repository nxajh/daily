### Anthropic：Claude用11天完成费马大定理的形式化机器验证证明

> 来源：[Hacker News](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem)

> 来源：[ArsTechnica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/once-popular-for-attacking-ai-ascii-smuggling-is-embraced-by-spammers/) / [TechMeme](https://www.techmeme.com/260905/p4#a260905p4)
